# Cancellation and advertising spend baseline contracts

Refs #236 and #239. This prerequisite repairs two missing event measures for P04-008 and P04-011; it does not close either detector card.

The event-only measures are `CANCELLED_ORDER_RATE` / `basisPoints` and `AD_SPEND` / `minorCNY`. `BaselineRequest.normalizedObservations` is an optional readonly array of exactly two strict concrete variants: STORE/SKU cancellation cohorts and CAMPAIGN spend aggregates. Old callers omit the field without a default or new discriminator. Existing P3 metrics/snapshots remain unchanged and cannot pretend to carry either event-only kind.

Both variants retain exact immutable reference/hash, binding, subject, definition, period, visibility, source quality and bounded evidence. Cancellation retains exact count/ratio and cohort sample size. Spend retains actual nullable `MetricMeasuredCoverage`, including its binding, interval, source version, observed time, coverage and evidence. VERIFIED spend requires exact full measurement coverage, a covered watermark, positive measurement sample size and known bounded spend. Verified measured zero is valid; absent measurement is not a verified zero. Missing exact provenance remains zero coverage with a typed limitation. Spend observations do not require attribution; an adapter must establish field-level spend reliability before separating attribution-only limitations.

A private two-case normalizer projects snapshots and the two observations into the existing source-neutral sample. The same exact dyadic median/MAD, weekly alignment, lookback/age cutoff, newest visible/computed revision, deduplication, immutable payload conflict checks, null states and coverage arithmetic serve all sources. Every timestamp in aggregate and measured-coverage payloads is compared as an instant; opaque IDs remain exact. Statistical sampleSize counts usable windows, while observation sampleSize counts cohort orders or distinct source measurements. Monetary median/MAD can be fractional and exceed 10000; cancellation remains bounded to probability limits.

Event-specific gates allow cancellation only for cancellation spikes. Spend baselines belong to spend spikes; current spend references may support both spend spikes and ROAS drops. Legacy metric evidence is not globally tightened. Downstream detectors must require the correct baseline measure independently.

Implementation sequence: contract RED/GREEN, normalizer RED/GREEN, decision-domain compatibility, then combined contracts/baseline regressions, build/typecheck, lint, formatting, full-function AST and secret checks. Prior cancellation commits remain intact; the companion has independent reviewable commits. Actual commands, results and final HEAD are recorded in the coordinator implementation report.

No public index, port signature, P3 calculator, database, runtime, provider or detector changes. Future adapters must verify aggregate references against real facts and deduplicate revisions. Actual orders without `cancelledAt` remain `SOURCE_TIME_MISSING`; ordered/paid/update/observation time cannot substitute. A campaign daily date does not establish a same-hour measured spend interval. This repair neither fabricates full-window coverage nor claims an actual normalized adapter or runtime integration. Both detector cards remain blocked until this prerequisite is merged.
