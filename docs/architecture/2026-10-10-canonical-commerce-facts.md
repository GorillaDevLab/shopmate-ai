# Typed canonical refund, inventory and campaign facts

The P2.5 storage foundation now includes `commerce.RefundListing`, `commerce.InventoryListing` and `commerce.CampaignMetricListing`. Each row belongs to the existing `SourceScope`, whose namespace binds organization, workspace, store, connection, external store, provider and generation. Compound foreign keys verify the row's organization/workspace/store ownership. Source version, source update time, payload hash, quality state/issues and ingestion time remain explicit typed columns. External store identity comes from the scope, as in canonical product and order storage.

Refund identity is `(scopeId, externalRefundId)`. All seven Connector refund statuses, CNY minor amounts, requested/succeeded timestamps and nullable SKU/succeeded facts are preserved. Amounts cannot exceed the request, and success cannot precede the request. Missing success facts on a succeeded refund require `REFUND_UNKNOWN`; contradictory success facts on another status require `SOURCE_INCONSISTENT`. These exceptions preserve only the Connector contract's anomalies, without disabling amount or chronology validation. Order and SKU IDs are literal facts: independently arriving streams do not require a canonical order or product foreign key.

Inventory identity is `(scopeId, externalSkuId, externalLocationId)`. Available, reserved, on-hand and safety stock remain nullable BIGINT quantities; lead time remains nullable and bounded to 0–365 days. Unknown available/reserved/on-hand facts require `INVENTORY_UNKNOWN`. When all three are known, available plus reserved cannot exceed on-hand. The addition uses NUMERIC before comparison to avoid overflow; `SOURCE_INCONSISTENT` does not bypass conservation. Null safety stock or lead time does not itself require an uncertainty issue, matching the contract.

Campaign metric identity is `(scopeId, externalCampaignId, date)`. PostgreSQL DATE stores a valid calendar day in years 0001–9999; the tests cover both endpoints, leap days and invalid calendar dates. Impressions, clicks, attributed orders, spend and attributed revenue remain nullable; any unknown metric requires `MARKETING_UNKNOWN`. Currency is CNY. The contract imposes no click/order funnel relationship, so storage preserves such provider observations without inventing a restriction.

All quantities and minor amounts are nonnegative and bounded by JavaScript's maximum safe integer, 9007199254740991. External identities use C collation and the existing validator's 256 UTF-16-unit budget. Canonically equivalent Unicode spellings remain separate literal keys. Source timestamps and refund timestamps use TIMESTAMPTZ(3) in years 0001–9999, with unknown source time requiring `SOURCE_TIME_MISSING`. As with order storage, PostgreSQL may round higher-precision timestamp input; strict input normalization belongs at a future ingestion boundary.

All three tables enable and force RLS. SELECT requires the established `commerce.can_read(scopeId)` current membership, workspace access, merchant, active connection and generation checks, plus `session_user = current_user`. Only the commerce reader receives SELECT, PUBLIC receives no table access, and source/sync workers receive no table DML. Existing internal validator execution stays revoked from PUBLIC. SQL role switching cannot impersonate an authorized reader session. No new function, ingestion RPC, source stream, STORE sync job or page progress behavior is introduced.

The mandatory tenant gate registers `canonical-commerce-facts.test.mjs`, using a separately guarded loopback `shopmate_tenant_test_facts` database. Real PostgreSQL checks cover statuses, unknown values, explicit anomaly exceptions, all safe numeric bounds, conservation overflow, literal Unicode/C collation, metadata/quality, compound ownership, atomic rollback, two tenants and a second store, reader/worker denial, membership revocation and connection generation reset. An actual Prisma migration diff must be empty. A CI workflow assertion protects this registration alongside existing product/order gates.

This delivers normalized durable storage only. Lease-fenced ingestion, page receipts and atomic cursor advancement, per-stream freshness, duplicate/out-of-order handling and full source-to-sync acceptance remain subsequent units of the approved P2.4–P2.5 plan. No deployment or online migration has been performed.
