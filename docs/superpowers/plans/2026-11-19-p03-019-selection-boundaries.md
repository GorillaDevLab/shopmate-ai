# Observed selection and authority boundaries

These refine the approved additive contract; no runtime authority is granted by parsing.

- STORE in existing EventEntity and MetricSubject is strict kind-only. The full shared five-part binding provides its identity.
- Measured TRAFFIC/CAMPAIGN_MEASUREMENT and windowed FEE intervals must fit the certified partition window. Traffic projection uses exact measurement interval. Nonmeasurement lifecycle/history may precede W or occur at W.end if actually observed by through; do not invent a universal occurredAt-in-W requirement. Future observation and future transaction/accepted/measurement timestamps still reject; already observed notices may forecast future ETA.
- A successful selection has matching singleton metric coverage for its projected collections. Nullable unknown fields retain finite reasons and non-VERIFIED status. Missing/unsupported projections can return SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE, never fabricate empty arrays or allocation.
- STORE financial selection can resolve actual per-SKU cost/refund/return lines, preserving original order identity and amounts. FEE/TRAFFIC require their exact subject. Exact CAMPAIGN selection keeps its campaign identity; SKU fee/campaign allocation unavailable.
- STORE ROAS is an explicit narrow existing-behavior exception; see [the STORE ROAS proof](2026-11-19-p03-019-store-roas-proof.md). All campaign components and all base MARKETING references are needed, original per-campaign values/window/evidence retained. No generic STORE-labeled campaign observation or SKU traffic sum. Preserve generic DTO kind/subject vocabulary. Component coverage uses the declared explicit10000/source-quality attestation and known measured<=eligible; do not infer an unfrozen equality or formula from counters.
- BaseRef has hashes/stream/page IDs, not original bytes. Schema can check structural stream reachability and SKU presence in supplied original order lines, not authenticate original order amounts or campaign universe. Authorized reader/adapter must resolve and verify exact sealed originals and authoritative complete source set. Do not claim DB/crypto/live membership proof from supplied graph parsing.
- An append request has no nested certificate; service must compare its fields and every source byte against the separately resolved authoritative certificate under its actual producer/model. Structural append parsing does not replace that comparison.
- Stored fact recordedAt and origin receipt recordedAt are independent canonical DB/security samples; atomicity does not require literal equality. No publication-to-capture clockRevision order rule is frozen, so keep bounded values pinned in the sealed graph without inventing order. Never compare security timestamps to adjustable business visibleAt. Runtime publisher/reader certify the actual current business clock.
- Ordinary observed projection outer sourceVersion and observedAt retain the immutable observed source. Campaign measuredCoverage retains that same source identity and its actual interval; the original measurementVersion remains reachable in the captured fact. Connector data.source metadata comes from authenticated original base records, not a forged replacement.
- SKU refunds without a direct SKU ID need an authentic same-refund and same-line allocation retained in projection; a multi-line order alone does not prove a refund allocation.
- Inventory extension composition preserves actual INVENTORY_POSITION asOf/quarantine/inbound/safetyBufferHours as primary, with actual same-SKU/location visible SUPPLIER_NOTICE providing promisedLeadHours/newEta when supported. Do not replace/sum a current inbound position with a historical notice quantity, or invent an asOf from a notice. Missing measurements remain null; canonical historical source choice is the authorized adapter's pinned-mapping obligation.
- Legacy MetricCampaignFact validates campaign day using its frozen fixed+08 semantics. Historical named-zone observations that cannot be projected losslessly into that legacy input can remain typed unavailable until a supported versioned mapper exists; never retime or modify the original facts or old validator to force a match.
- VERIFIED projection sourceWatermark must be at least periodEnd, consistent with existing complete-window snapshot/compute expectations. Actual authenticated source-watermark derivation remains the reader/adapter obligation where BaseRef only contains opaque source references.
- The current exact-W traffic proof has no disjoint-cohort or method-selection witness. Existing funnel-marketing conflicts() rejects overlapping source intervals. Distinct selected same-subject/exact-W traffic measurements therefore cannot be combined or arbitrarily reduced into a VERIFIED projection; reject ambiguous proof/fail typed unavailable or conflict. Correction versions remain handled by immutable latest-version selection. Genuine certified zero-row traffic still valid. Generic legacy input types remain unchanged.
