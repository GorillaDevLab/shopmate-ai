# P03-019 STORE ROAS selection clarification

**Decision.** Admit STORE/ROAS in v1 using the existing three-field selection, the complete campaign component partitions in the sealed store-domain capture and its base MARKETING receipt set. STORE is `{kind:"STORE"}`; its identity comes from the common five-part binding. Every component retains its original CAMPAIGN identity. This is a narrowly defined aggregate mapping from campaign-day measurements to STORE ROAS. The calculator retains responsibility for summing original minor amounts and dividing once.

**Compatibility.** At base `26c847c4d0988dd0215c5a124f8b7ebcf95b7d3d`, `apps/api/src/metrics/funnel-marketing-metrics.ts` selects every campaign row for STORE, selects a specific campaign by ID for CAMPAIGN, and explicitly rejects SKU allocation in `campaignSide`. Its `globalCampaignsKnown` requires full applicable measurement and known amounts for each row, `matchingCohorts` matches intervals for rows on the same day, `conflicts` rejects duplicated campaign/date keys and `proofCoverage` requires a matching STORE ROAS proof including campaignFacts. `packages/contracts/src/metrics/metric.schema.ts` admits these component rows under STORE metricCoverage and validates scope, time, window and source evidence.

## P03-019 bounded graph validation

For a **complete** STORE ROAS selection, apply the ordinary exact binding/generation/window/visibleAt/revision/definition checks and require the following additional conditions.

1. Request, selection and singleton metricCoverage use STORE. The requested kind and coverage kind are ROAS, with the campaignFacts collection covered at 10000. Require requested capture domain CAMPAIGN_MEASUREMENT and the sealed, scope-matching four-stream base including MARKETING. Read the components from the complete capture graph supplied in the selection-proof envelope, independently of the caller's proposed selectionReceiptIds. Keep existing bounds and use sets/maps, indexed by structured tuples.
2. Enumerate **all applicable CAMPAIGN_MEASUREMENT coverage partitions** in that store capture for the requested W. For a complete proof each has its own exact CAMPAIGN subject and COMPLETE/10000 membership chain from its certified snapshot. Any applicable partial/unavailable component precludes complete STORE proof. The union of those chains supplies the required observed membership IDs. Use the lineage rules to resolve selected fact versions and their immutable origins. Other domains' receipts, superseded origin-only members and receipts for another window are ineligible as current campaign measurements.
3. Require the selection's receipt witness to cover the exact union of those current component page receipt IDs and **all** sealed base MARKETING page IDs; base and observed references must resolve unambiguously. Maintain the root evidence chain through the singleton metricCoverage/input evidence to these IDs; a component row references its own membership and corresponding authenticated base source evidence. Unrelated source evidence already carried by the overall capture is retained in that capture. The input never substitutes a STORE-labeled observed campaign fact or a synthetic combined campaign row.
4. Require a bijection between selected campaign-day source measurements and projected campaignFacts by `(externalCampaignId,businessDate)`, with distinct keys, matching binding/external store, source evidence, measurement version/sourceVersion lineage, actual observedAt and cutoff, plus original spendMinor and attributedRevenueMinor. Each selected source subject equals its own payload and projected data campaign ID. For a complete projection, both amounts and counters are known, with `measuredCount <= eligibleCount`. The source explicitly supplies 10000 basis points and complete measurement quality, backed by authoritative measurement attestation. The counter ratio does not define coverage. Each projected measuredCoverage retains its source receipt/version and actual interval. The interval equals the intersection of the requested W and the named Asia/Shanghai day, closes by observation, and agrees with all other component intervals on that date. A domain/subject coverage partition still uses the exact W required by the capture protocol. Every component row preserves its original date and measurement fields.
5. An empty list or a zero-member terminal partition cannot supply a measured STORE ROAS by itself. A genuine measured component with spend 0 may be represented; the calculator retains its existing zero-denominator/null rule if total spend is zero. Missing scale/coverage stays unavailable or partial as appropriate. Exact CAMPAIGN selection continues to require the requested campaign ID. SKU ROAS attribution remains unsupported. Distinct STORE traffic requires its own observed STORE measurement; summing SKU counters cannot establish it. STORE-to-SKU traffic relabeling, estimated fee allocation and proportional campaign splits remain outside this exception.

## P03-022/P03-027 authority

A BaseRef carries page IDs/hashes, not the underlying records. Structural parsing can discover a component omitted from a selection **when that component is in the supplied capture**. It cannot discover an omitted genuine campaign if someone fabricates a self-consistent replacement capture and page set.

The authorized reader must supply an unchanged, hash-verified complete-domain store capture at the requested cutoff. The adapter must resolve the entire exact base MARKETING receipt set under the same current authority, decode its original campaign-day rows, and compare its eligible campaign-day key set for W with the sealed observed component set and projected rows. Resolve source partitions for every applicable campaign/day in the certified model/domain scope, including explicit missing/partial cases. Where the sealed model and base disagree about the universe, keys are missing, a component is unavailable, or bounds prevent complete enumeration, the adapter cannot return a complete STORE proof. Return the existing typed unavailable result when no authorized complete projection can be supplied. Carry authentic base fields such as impressions, clicks, attributedOrders and connector source metadata from the base records, and the measured spend/revenue/interval from their authenticated source mapping. A placeholder base page or schema-valid receipt ID is insufficient authority. This same authorized graph can support STORE ROAS without adding a wire variant or coupling the parser to database/crypto.
